100% Local Privacy

Privacy Policy - Green CRM

Last updated: November 2026

1. 100% Local and Private Architecture

Green CRM is built on a strict local-first privacy principle:

  • Zero external servers: All your contacts, pipeline stages, private notes, quick replies, and reminders are stored exclusively in your browser using IndexedDB and chrome.storage.local.
  • Zero telemetry or trackers: The extension contains no analytics, tracking scripts, or session recorders.
  • No bulk scraping: Green CRM does not extract chat lists or read messages in the background. It only reads the active chat name when you click on a conversation.

2. Required Permissions

  • sidePanel: Opens the CRM side panel next to WhatsApp Web.
  • storage: Saves local extension preferences.
  • alarms and notifications: Triggers scheduled reminder notifications on your computer.
  • https://web.whatsapp.com/*: Detects the selected chat and inserts message text when you click insert.
  • https://api.gumroad.com/*: Used only when you submit a Pro license key to verify its validity.

3. Data Control

You can export a full JSON backup or export your contacts to CSV from the Settings panel at any time. Uninstalling the extension deletes all locally stored data from your browser.
